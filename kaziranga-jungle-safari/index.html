About Kaziranga National Park

Kaziranga National Park in Assam is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a Tiger Reserve, famous for holding the largest population of the Indian one-horned rhinoceros in the world. The park's tall elephant grass, wetlands and woodland also shelter wild Asian elephants, wild water buffalo, swamp deer, hog deer, tigers and a very large number of resident and migratory birds.

A jeep safari is the best way to see the park. Safaris run in separate ranges — the Central (Kohora), Western (Bagori), Eastern (Agoratoli) and Burapahar ranges — each with its own landscape and wildlife. Wildlife you can see in Kaziranga

Kaziranga is best known for the Indian one-horned rhinoceros, which is seen on most safaris. The park also has wild Asian elephants, wild water buffalo, swamp deer (barasingha), hog deer, and a healthy tiger population that is seen only occasionally. Bird lovers can look for Bengal florican, swamp francolin, pelicans, storks, adjutants and migratory ducks in the cooler months.

Kaziranga safari zones explained

  • Central Range (Kohora): the main and most visited range, with open grassland and wetlands where rhinos are commonly seen.
  • Western Range (Bagori): known for a high density of rhinos and good wetland birdlife.
  • Eastern Range (Agoratoli): quieter, with large wetlands and migratory birds.
  • Burapahar Range: more forested and less crowded, a good choice if you have done the other ranges.

What to carry

Carry the original ID you uploaded (Aadhaar for Indian guests; passport or another valid ID for foreign guests), comfortable clothes in muted colours such as green, brown or khaki, a hat, sunscreen, mosquito repellent, drinking water, binoculars and a camera. Mornings can be cool from November to February, so bring a light jacket.

Season, getting there and pickup

Kaziranga is generally open for safaris from about November to April, when the park is dry enough for jeeps. It is closed during the monsoon (roughly May to October). The exact opening dates are decided each year by the forest department, so tell us your travel dates and we will confirm.

Kaziranga (Kohora) is on NH-715 in Assam. The nearest large airport is Guwahati (roughly 5 hours by road); Jorhat airport is closer. We arrange Guwahati airport / railway station ⇄ Kaziranga pickup and drop with fixed fares.

From Guwahati to Kaziranga (Kohora) is roughly 190–220 km and takes about 5 hours by road via NH-715, depending on traffic. Guwahati airport is the main gateway; Jorhat airport is nearer if you are coming from upper Assam. Our pickup & drop service runs Kaziranga ⇄ Guwahati airport / railway station at fixed fares.
