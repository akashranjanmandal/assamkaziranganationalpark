About Manas National Park

Manas National Park lies in the Himalayan foothills of Assam on the border with Bhutan. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, a Tiger Reserve, an Elephant Reserve and a Biosphere Reserve. Grassland, forest and river habitats support tigers, Asian elephants, greater one-horned rhinoceros, wild water buffalo, the rare golden langur, the pygmy hog and a rich variety of birds, including the Bengal florican.

Jeep safaris run in several zones — Bansbari, Bhuyan Para, Kahitama and Kokilabari, plus a Bansbari to Panbang (Bhutan) day trip for Indian guests. We help you choose a zone and arrange your safari booking and ID uploads in one simple form.

Manas shared jeep safari

Travelling solo or as a couple? Join a shared jeep: ₹1,500 per person for Indian guests and ₹2,200 per person for foreign guests (foreigner entry fee included). Shared jeeps run in the Bansbari Zone, with 1–3 adults per booking.

Wildlife you can see in Manas

Manas is one of the few places where you can see the rare golden langur, along with the pygmy hog, Asian elephant, greater one-horned rhinoceros, wild water buffalo, Bengal tiger and the endangered Bengal florican. More than 450 bird species have been recorded, including hornbills, giant kingfishers and many forest birds, and the Manas River and the Bhutan hills make a beautiful backdrop.

Manas safari zones explained

  • Bansbari Zone: the most popular zone for a first safari, with grassland and forest edges.
  • Bhuyan Para Range: a quieter range with forest and river scenery.
  • Kahitama Zone: a longer, more remote drive for travellers who want fewer vehicles.
  • Kokilabari Zone: a whole-day safari for guests who want a full day in the park.
  • Bansbari to Panbang (Bhutan): a day trip across the border into Bhutan, for Indian guests only.

Camera charges

Manas National Park charges a fee per camera, added to your booking total: still camera ₹200 (Indian) / ₹1,000 (foreigner); video camera ₹10,000 (Indian) / ₹20,000 (foreigner).

How to book your Manas safari

  1. Open the Manas safari booking form and pick private or shared, your zone, date and time.
  2. Add every guest's name and age and upload ID documents (Aadhaar for Indian guests; passport or other ID for foreign guests).
  3. Submit the request — no payment on the website. We check availability and send a secure payment link on WhatsApp.
  4. Pay online and receive your confirmation, with vehicle and driver details shared before the safari.

What to carry

Carry the original ID you uploaded (Aadhaar for Indian guests; passport or another valid ID for foreign guests), comfortable clothes in muted colours, a hat, sunscreen, mosquito repellent, drinking water, binoculars and a camera. Remember that Manas charges a fee per camera, which is added to your booking total. Mornings can be cool from November to February, so bring a light jacket.

Season, getting there and pickup

Manas is generally open for safaris from about November to April, with the park closed during the monsoon months. Dates are fixed by the forest department each year, so tell us your travel dates and we will confirm.

Manas is in the Bodoland (BTR) region of western Assam, on the Bhutan border. Barpeta Road and New Bongaigaon are the nearest railway stations and Guwahati is the nearest major airport (roughly 4 hours by road). We arrange Guwahati, Bongaigaon, Barpeta Road and Kaziranga pickup and drop to Manas with fixed fares.

From Guwahati to Manas is roughly 140–160 km and takes about 3.5–4 hours by road, depending on traffic and the zone you are visiting. Barpeta Road and New Bongaigaon are the nearest railway stations. Our pickup & drop service covers Guwahati, Bongaigaon, Barpeta Road and Kaziranga to Manas at fixed fares.
