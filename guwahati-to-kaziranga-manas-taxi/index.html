Pickup & drop — frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Guwahati airport to Manas or Kaziranga?

Fares are fixed per vehicle and one way. Pickup and drop to Manas start from ₹2,000 and Kaziranga ⇄ Guwahati airport / railway station starts from ₹6,500. The full rate card is on this page.

Is the fare per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle, one way. For a pickup and a drop, book the trip both ways.

How do I book a pickup or drop?

Open the pickup & drop form, choose the route and vehicle, add your dates and contact number. We will contact you on WhatsApp to confirm. No payment is taken on the website.

How far is Guwahati airport from Kaziranga and Manas?

Guwahati to Kaziranga (Kohora) is roughly 190–220 km, about 5 hours by road. Guwahati to Manas is roughly 140–160 km, about 3.5–4 hours. Timings change with traffic and road conditions.

Which vehicles are available?

Swift Dzire, Ertiga, Innova, Tempo Traveller (17 and 26 seaters), Urbania and Winger on selected routes — see the table for the vehicle available on each route.

Can I combine the taxi with a safari booking?

Yes. Book your Kaziranga or Manas safari first, then add pickup and drop for the same dates.

Need a pickup or drop?

Choose your route and vehicle and send your request — we reply on WhatsApp.
