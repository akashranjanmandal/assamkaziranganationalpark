KAZIRANGA & MANAS JEEP SAFARI BOOKING

Assam Wild Safari helps you book a Kaziranga jeep safari and a Manas National Park jeep safari — two UNESCO World Heritage wildlife parks in North East India. Kaziranga is home to the Indian one-horned rhinoceros; Manas, on the Bhutan border, shelters tigers, elephants, the golden langur and the pygmy hog.

Choose a private jeep or a shared seat, pick your zone, date and time, and send your request online. We confirm availability and share a secure payment link on WhatsApp. Add airport pickup & drop, or explore our North East India tour packages covering Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, Bhutan and more.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Which is the best jungle safari in India for rhinos and tigers?

Kaziranga National Park in Assam is the best place in India to see the Indian one-horned rhinoceros, and it also has one of the highest tiger densities in the country. Manas National Park, on the Bhutan border, is better for elusive species such as the golden langur, pygmy hog and Bengal florican. Both are UNESCO World Heritage Sites and we book safaris in both.

How do I book a Kaziranga or Manas jeep safari?

Open the booking page, choose the park, private or shared jeep, zone, date and time, enter the guest details and upload ID documents. We check seat availability and send a secure payment link on WhatsApp. Your safari is confirmed once the payment is received.

How much does a jeep safari cost in Kaziranga and Manas?

Kaziranga private jeeps start from ₹3,600 per jeep and shared seats are ₹1,000 per person for Indian guests. Manas private jeeps start from ₹5,500 per jeep and shared seats are ₹1,500 per person. Foreign guests pay a higher rate that includes the foreigner entry fee. Full rate cards are on the Kaziranga and Manas pages.

What is the best time to visit Kaziranga and Manas?

Both parks are generally open from about November to April and closed during the monsoon. Dates are fixed each year by the forest department.

Do you offer North East India tour packages?

Yes — ready itineraries across Manas, Kaziranga, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh and Bhutan, plus customised routes, with vehicle and pickup and drop arranged.

Can you arrange pickup and drop from Guwahati airport?

Yes. We run fixed-fare pickup and drop between Guwahati airport / railway station, Bongaigaon, Barpeta Road and Manas or Kaziranga.

How far is Guwahati from Kaziranga and Manas?

Guwahati to Kaziranga (Kohora) is roughly 190–220 km, about 5 hours by road. Guwahati to Manas is roughly 140–160 km, about 3.5–4 hours. We run fixed-fare pickup and drop from Guwahati airport and railway station.

Is there an elephant safari in Manas and Kaziranga?

Early-morning elephant rides are run by the forest department, subject to availability and park rules. Several of our itineraries include an optional early elephant ride before the jeep safari. Tell us your dates and we will check availability and the fee.

How can I get Kaziranga or Manas safari tickets?

Send a safari request on our booking page with your park, zone, date and time. We check seat availability and send a secure payment link on WhatsApp; nothing is paid when you send the request.
