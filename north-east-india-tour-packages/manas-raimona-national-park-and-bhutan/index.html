Manas, Raimona National Park & Bhutan — 5 Nights / 6 Days

Offbeat · Wildlife + Bhutan

Route: New Bongaigaon → Raimona → Gelephu → Manas → Panbang → New Bongaigaon

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1: Arrival at New Bongaigaon → Raimona National Park

Arrival at New Bongaigaon railway station and transfer to Raimona National Park (2 hours). After lunch, jeep safari. Halt.

Day 2: Transfer to Gelephu, Bhutan

After breakfast, transfer to Gelephu, Bhutan (91 km, 2.5 hours). Halt.

Day 3: Gelephu sightseeing → Manas

Gelephu local sightseeing. After lunch, transfer to Manas. Halt.

Day 4: Jeep safari to Panbang, Bhutan

Morning jeep safari to Panbang, Bhutan, inside Manas National Park and Royal Manas, Bhutan. Halt at Bhutan river side accommodation.

Day 5: Twin waterfalls & hanging bridge

Morning visit to the twin waterfalls and hanging bridge. After lunch, back to Manas National Park and jeep safari inside the national park. Halt at Manas.

Day 6: Elephant ride (optional) & Kakoijana

Morning elephant ride (optional). After breakfast, Kakoijana forest (sure sighting of the endangered golden langur). After lunch, drop at New Bongaigaon railway station (15 km) for the Saraighat or Vande Bharat Express. Tour end.

Good to know

For Indian tourists only. Do not forget to carry your original Voter ID card and Aadhaar card.
