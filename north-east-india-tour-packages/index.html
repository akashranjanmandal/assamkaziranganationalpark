North East tour packages — frequently asked questions

What North East India tour packages do you offer?

We offer 7 ready itineraries — including wildlife tours of Manas and Kaziranga, Meghalaya (Shillong, Dawki, Mawlynnong, Cherrapunji), Arunachal Pradesh (Tawang, Anini) and Bhutan-side trips — and we can customise any route.

Can I customise my North East itinerary?

Yes. Pick one of our itineraries as a starting point or describe your own plan with the number of days and nights, and we will send you a quote.

How do I book a North East tour?

Choose an itinerary, press Book this itinerary and send your name and mobile number. We contact you on WhatsApp to confirm. Nothing is paid on the website when you send a request.

Do I need a permit for Arunachal Pradesh?

Indian tourists need an Inner Line Permit (ILP) for Arunachal Pradesh. Rules for foreign nationals are different — please ask us before you plan.

Do you arrange airport and railway station pickup?

Yes. We arrange pickup and drop from Guwahati and other airports and railway stations, with fixed fares for Manas and Kaziranga routes.

Want your own route?

Tell us the places, days and vehicle — we will plan and quote it.
