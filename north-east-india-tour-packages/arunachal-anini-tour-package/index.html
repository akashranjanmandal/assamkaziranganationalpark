Arunachal Anini Tour Package — 5 Nights / 6 Days

Arunachal Pradesh

Route: Dibrugarh Airport → Roing → Anini → Roing → Dibrugarh Airport

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1: Dibrugarh Airport pickup → Roing

Upon arrival at Dibrugarh Airport, meet our driver and proceed towards Roing, Arunachal Pradesh, via Tinsukia and Dhola–Sadiya Bridge, depending on the selected route and road conditions. Enjoy the scenic journey with suitable breaks for lunch, refreshments and relaxation. On arrival in Roing, check in to the hotel, enjoy dinner and overnight stay. Night stay: Roing. Approximate distance: 150–180 km.

Day 2: Roing → Mayodia Pass → Hunli → Anini

After an early breakfast, proceed towards Anini through the beautiful mountain roads of Mayodia Pass and Hunli. Enjoy breathtaking mountain views, lush green forests, misty landscapes and photo stops at safe locations, subject to weather and road conditions. Arrive in Anini, check in to the hotel, enjoy dinner and relax after the long mountain drive. Night stay: Anini. Approximate distance: 200–235 km. Travel note: approximately 7–9 hours or longer, depending on road conditions.

Day 3: Anini local sightseeing

After breakfast, explore Anini town and its surrounding natural beauty: Anini town exploration, scenic mountain viewpoints, beautiful valleys and nature photography, nearby local villages or cultural spots (subject to accessibility and permission) and leisure time to enjoy the peaceful surroundings. Return to the hotel in the afternoon and enjoy a relaxed evening. Night stay: Anini.

Day 4: Anini – Dibang Valley nature excursion

After breakfast, enjoy a relaxed day exploring the scenic surroundings of Anini and Dibang Valley: visit accessible valley viewpoints, enjoy panoramic mountain views, explore nearby riverbanks or village areas (subject to road access) and nature photography. Return to the hotel for lunch and relaxation. The sightseeing schedule remains flexible depending on weather, road conditions and local accessibility. Night stay: Anini.

Day 5: Anini → Hunli → Mayodia Pass → Roing

After breakfast, check out and begin the return journey towards Roing via Hunli and Mayodia Pass. Enjoy the beautiful mountain scenery along the way, with suitable breaks for refreshments and rest. Upon arrival in Roing, check in to the hotel and relax. Dinner and overnight stay. Night stay: Roing. Travel note: allow approximately 7–9 hours or longer, depending on road and weather conditions.

Day 6: Roing → Dibrugarh Airport drop

After breakfast, check out and proceed towards Dibrugarh Airport. Enjoy the return drive with suitable refreshment breaks. Arrive at the airport for your onward journey according to your flight schedule. Tour ends with beautiful memories of Arunachal Pradesh.

Tour highlights

  • Roing, Mayodia Pass, Hunli, Anini Town, Dibang Valley and scenic mountain landscapes

Package summary

  • Duration: 5 Nights / 6 Days
  • Pickup point: Dibrugarh Airport
  • Drop point: Dibrugarh Airport
  • Roing stay: 2 nights
  • Anini stay: 3 nights
  • Main attractions: Mayodia Pass, Hunli, Anini Town, Dibang Valley and scenic mountain landscapes
  • Tour type: Private tour with a comfortable and relaxed travel schedule

Important notes

  • Arunachal Pradesh Inner Line Permit (ILP) must be arranged in advance for Indian tourists.
  • Road conditions, weather and local restrictions may affect travel schedules and sightseeing.
  • Mayodia Pass and remote sightseeing locations are subject to accessibility and prevailing conditions.
  • Guests are advised to carry warm clothing, personal medicines, drinking water and essential travel items.
  • Hotel availability and meal arrangements should be confirmed before the tour.
  • The itinerary may be adjusted for guest comfort and safety.
  • Flight timings should be checked before confirming the pickup and drop schedule.

Good to know

Recommended travel plan: this itinerary is designed to reduce travel fatigue by keeping two overnight stays in Roing and three nights in Anini, allowing guests to enjoy the mountain journey and local sightseeing at a comfortable pace.
