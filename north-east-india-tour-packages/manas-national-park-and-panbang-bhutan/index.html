Manas National Park & Panbang (Bhutan) — 3 Nights / 4 Days

Wildlife + Bhutan

Route: Manas National Park → Panbang (Bhutan) → Manas

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1: Receive at Manas National Park

Overnight at Manas.

Day 2: Jeep safari to Panbang

Morning jeep safari through Manas National Park and Royal Manas up to Panbang, Bhutan. Overnight at Panbang (river side room).

Day 3: Twin waterfalls & jungle safari

Visit the twin waterfalls. After lunch, jungle safari back through the national park. Overnight at Manas.

Day 4: Check out

After breakfast, check out.
