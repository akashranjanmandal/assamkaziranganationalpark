Guwahati, Kaziranga & Tawang (Arunachal Pradesh) — 6 Nights / 7 Days

Arunachal Circuit

Route: Guwahati → Bomdila/Shergaon → Tawang → Dirang → Bhalukpong → Guwahati

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1: Guwahati → Bomdila / Shergaon

Guwahati to Bomdila or Shergaon via Kalaktang. Stay at Shergaon or Bomdila. 300 km (8 hours).

Day 2: Bomdila → Tawang via Sela Pass

Bomdila to Tawang, 180 km (6 hours), via Sela Pass. Stay at Tawang.

Day 3: Bumla Pass & Sungester Lake

Visit Bumla Pass and Sungester Lake. Evening back to Tawang. Stay at Tawang.

Day 4: Tawang → Dirang (Sangti Valley)

Tawang to Dirang (Sangti Valley), 140 km (5 hours). Stay at Dirang.

Day 5: Dirang → Bhalukpong

Dirang to Bhalukpong, 150 km (4 hours). Stay at Bhalukpong.

Day 6: Bhalukpong → Guwahati

Bhalukpong to Guwahati, 270 km (5 hours). Stay at Guwahati.

Day 7: Maa Kamakhya darshan & drop

Morning Maa Kamakhya darshan and drop at Guwahati airport or railway station. Tour end.
