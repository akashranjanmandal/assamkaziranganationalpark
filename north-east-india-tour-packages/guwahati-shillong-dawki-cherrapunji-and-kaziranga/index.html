Route: Guwahati → Shillong → Dawki → Cherrapunji → Kaziranga → Guwahati

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1: Pickup from Guwahati → Shillong

Pickup from Guwahati airport or railway station. Transfer to Shillong. Overnight at Shillong.

Day 2: Shillong local sightseeing

Shillong local sightseeing. Stay at Shillong.

Day 3: Shillong → Dawki → Mawlynnong → Cherrapunji

Shillong to Dawki and Mawlynnong village, then transfer to Cherrapunji. Stay at Cherrapunji.

Day 4: Cherrapunji local sightseeing

Cherrapunji local sightseeing. Stay at Cherrapunji.

Day 5: Cherrapunji → Kaziranga

Cherrapunji to Kaziranga. Stay at Kaziranga.

Day 6: Elephant ride & jeep safari

Morning 6 AM elephant ride. After breakfast, jeep safari. After lunch, transfer to Guwahati. Stay at Guwahati.

Day 7: Maa Kamakhya darshan & drop

Morning Maa Kamakhya darshan and drop at Guwahati airport or railway station. Tour end.
