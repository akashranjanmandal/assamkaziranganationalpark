UNESCO World Heritage Site

KAZIRANGA
NATIONAL PARK

Kaziranga is a vast, flood-prone landscape dominated by the Brahmaputra River — the last bastion for the Great Indian One-Horned Rhinoceros. A mix of dense elephant grass, marshland, and tropical moist broadleaf forests. Home to two-thirds of the world's one-horned rhino population.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

MANAS
NATIONAL PARK

Located in the foothills of the Eastern Himalayas, Manas provides a unique ecological transition zone — critical habitat for endangered species found nowhere else on earth. The Manas River and its alluvial fans create a landscape of extraordinary biodiversity and raw beauty.
