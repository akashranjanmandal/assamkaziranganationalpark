How this works

A short guide for running the admin day to day.

What happens when a guest books
  1. The guest picks a park, zone, date and time, enters everyone's details and uploads their ID (Manas guests also choose cameras, which are added to the total), and submits. Nothing is paid at this point — it arrives as a New request under Safari Bookings and the guest gets an email saying it was received.
  2. Check whether seats are available for the slot. If they are, open the booking, check the amount (it is worked out from the rate card, you can change it) and press Seats available — send payment link on WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens with the message, the breakdown and the payment link already written — you just press send. If there are no seats, press Seats not available to message the guest instead.
  3. When the guest pays through the link, the booking turns Paid & confirmed by itself. Open it and press Send confirmation on WhatsApp to send the confirmation message.
  4. About 2 days before the safari, enter the vehicle number and driver and press Send driver details on WhatsApp.
What the statuses mean
New requestThe guest submitted a booking. Check the seats and send the payment link (or tell them there are none).
Waiting for paymentYou sent the payment link. It turns Paid & confirmed by itself when the guest pays.
Paid & confirmedPayment received. The safari is booked.
Needs reviewAn older booking waiting for you to check the payment. Check it, then press Confirm booking.
CancelledCancelled by you. Refunds are made from the Razorpay dashboard.
Good to know
  • Prices: change them under Prices & Zones. Old bookings keep the price they were booked at.
  • Jeeps: a private jeep takes up to 5 guests. The website works out how many jeeps a group needs and charges per jeep.
  • Aadhaar cards are private. Only this admin page can open them.
  • Itinerary bookings and car bookings are requests only (no payment on the website): the guest sends their contact details and what they want. You contact them, agree the price, then press Confirm. Manage the itineraries themselves under Itineraries.
  • WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp with the message already written — you just press send. Automatic confirmations are sent by the system itself.
  • Refunds are done in the Razorpay dashboard. After refunding, mark the booking Cancelled here so your list stays correct.
